Created by a NEBOSH & OSHA trained HSE professional · "To all moms by a mom"
Evidence base: general agricultural safety guidance comparable to established occupational farm-safety frameworks such as HSE agriculture guidance · WHO guidance on pesticide safety and storage · general fencing and barrier safety principles for open water features · general occupational guidance on machinery guarding and confined-space/engulfment awareness
This checklist is educational general farm and rural household safety guidance to help families build strong safety habits around machinery, water, animals and chemicals. It is not professional agricultural machinery operation training, veterinary guidance, or a substitute for local regulations. If you have concerns about specific machinery operation, animal handling, chemical use, or structural safety on your property, please consult a qualified agricultural safety professional, equipment manufacturer, or your local regulations.
